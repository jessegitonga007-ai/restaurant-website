A Nairobi salon built around detail, care, and quiet luxury.

Glamor Nails & Salon began as a small beauty corner for clients who wanted refined nail work without rushing the appointment. Today, the studio brings together nails, hair, skin care, and massage in one polished beauty experience.

Experience & Certifications
